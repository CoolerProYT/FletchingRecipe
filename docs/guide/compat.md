# Other mods

All of these are optional. JEI and Arrow+ work on Fabric and NeoForge; More Fletching Tables is a Fabric mod.

## JEI

[JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) gets two recipe categories. Both list the fletching table as their crafting station.

- **Fletching**: every fletching recipe, including datapack recipes, and a tipped arrow recipe for every potion.
- **Explosive Fletching**: every explosive ingredient combined with plain, spectral and tipped arrows.

Click the arrow in the fletching table screen to open both categories. JEI also lists explosive arrows as separate items for each ingredient.

JEI uses the server's recipes and the server's amounts from the [common config](../config/common). The explosive ingredients come from the player's own [explosive ingredient config](../config/explosive-ingredients).

## Arrow+

With [Arrow+](https://modrinth.com/mod/arrow+) installed, its arrows can be [tipped](./tipped-arrows) and made [explosive](./explosive-arrows) at the fletching table, and JEI lists those crafts too.

## More Fletching Tables

[More Fletching Tables](https://modrinth.com/mod/more-fletching-tables) (Fabric) adds more fletching table variants. They all open the Fletching Recipe screen and show up as crafting stations in JEI.

To use an item on one of those tables instead, sneak while right-clicking with the item in your hand.
